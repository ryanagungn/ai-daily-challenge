The new ultra-lightweight laptop delivers phenomenal battery life, easily lasting over 14 hours on a single charge during heavy multitasking. The OLED display is vibrant and sharp with true blacks. However, the fan noise gets noticeably loud during intensive 4K video rendering, and having only two USB-C ports without a full-sized HDMI or SD card slot requires carrying multiple dongles. Overall, it is a solid 4.5/5 choice for digital nomads and developers, provided they upgrade their adapters.
